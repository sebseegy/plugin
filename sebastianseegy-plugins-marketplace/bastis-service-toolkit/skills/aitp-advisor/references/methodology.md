# AITP Methodology: Assess → Align → Adopt

## Phase 1: ASSESS (20–25% of timeline)

**Mission:** Build a strategic framework by uncovering pain points. Move from "What do you want?" → "Where is the friction?"

### Objective
Identify manual bottlenecks where AI can have the greatest impact. Align on scope and define what success looks like before any build begins.

### Discovery Questions
**Manual bottlenecks:** What manual, repetitive tasks take up the most time for your team?
**Process complexity:** Are there processes that would benefit from auto-categorization, summarization, or data extraction?
**Business impact:** What information do your teams wish they had at their fingertips? How do you want AI to boost workflow productivity?
**Product alignment:** What's your familiarity with monday.com's AI capabilities (Sidekick, AI Blocks, etc.)?

### Impact Areas (anchor client goals here)
- Cutting out manual work: freeing up teams from repetitive clicking and data entry
- Scaling without adding headcount: handling more volume without more people
- Improving visibility: turning messy data into clear, actionable insights for leadership
- Making faster decisions: using AI to surface the right info at the right time
- Empowering non-technical teams: letting anyone build or manage complex workflows using natural language
- Consolidating tech stacks: replacing expensive third-party tools with built-in AI capabilities

### Assess Checklist (before moving to Align)
- Identified at least 3 manual bottlenecks
- Know the customer's current state AND ideal future work state
- Can link each bottleneck to a specific AI tool (Vibe, Agents, Workflows, Sidekick, AI Blocks)
- Assessed how comfortable/tech-savvy the customer is with AI
- Customer understands AI is a tool for efficiency, not a total replacement for human decision-making

### Deliverables
1. Current state process map (Canvas or similar)
2. AI opportunity mapping: which AI capabilities apply to which workflows
3. Prioritized use case list aligned with package scope
4. Updated project plan with milestones

## Phase 2: ALIGN (45–55% of timeline — the engine of the project)

**Mission:** Design and build the AI-powered solution tailored to the customer's specific goals. "Project-based advisory" — configure core AI capabilities to solve the problems identified in Assess.

### Timeline breakdown
- Solution Design: 1–2 sessions per use case to map the logic
- POC Build & Demo: 1–2 weeks per use case ("skeleton" build)
- AI Configuration & Build: 2–4 weeks (depending on tier) to finalize the full environment
- Testing & Validation: 3–5 days per use case to ensure AI logic holds up against real-world data

### Expectations
- **Target-State Design:** Map out the new workflow — exactly where AI, board structures, and automations will live
- **Iterative POCs:** Build "skeleton" models and demo early. Don't wait until the end for feedback
- **AI Configuration:** Set up and "train" tools — define Sidekick's context, refine AI Block prompts with 15–20+ test items, build AI workflows
- **Validation Leadership:** Lead hands-on testing with process owners and 2–3 end users using real-world scenarios
- **Technical Escalation:** If build requires complex Vibe coding or advanced architecture, trigger a TC request
- **Documentation:** Record as you build — track prompt designs, automation logic, and board structures for smooth handoff

### Validation Questions (shift from discovery → validation)
- **Goal Alignment:** "Are we still aligned on our top priorities and the business goals we set at the start?"
- **Design Feedback:** "Now that you see the blueprint, does this solution design meet your expectations?"
- **Testing Output:** "During this walkthrough, is the AI producing the specific results you were hoping for?"
- **Functionality & Edge Cases:** "Does the AI output look correct, and how should it handle unusual data?"
- **User Experience:** "Does the workflow feel intuitive for your team, or is there still friction?"

### Align Checklist (before moving to Adopt)
- Priority Check: still aligned on core goals?
- Design Approval: solution design meets expectations for how work should flow?
- Core Output: AI consistently producing expected results from testing?
- Edge Cases: tested how AI handles unusual/unpredictable data inputs?
- Reliability: confident in AI accuracy based on testing sessions?
- Intuitiveness: workflow feels natural, no friction for the team?
- End-to-End Logic: all AI features work seamlessly together start to finish?
- Stakeholder Buy-In: process owners AND end-users have personally tested the build?
- PBO Mapping: can you clearly show how the build hits the Positive Business Outcomes?

### Outcomes before moving to Adopt
- Proven Functionality: every agreed use case is built and works as planned
- AI Reliability: all AI components stress-tested and consistently produce correct results
- Customer Validation: customer has tested the solution themselves and confirmed it solves their problems
- Documentation Ready: clear guide covering automation logic and AI prompt designs
- Formal Sign-Off: formal "thumbs up" received via email, project plan updated

## Phase 3: ADOPT (25–30% of timeline)

**Mission:** Launch with confidence and ensure long-term success. Bridge the gap between "functional software" and "measurable ROI."

### Timeline breakdown
- Go-Live & Adoption Support (1–2 weeks): real-time support during initial rollout, troubleshoot, ensure adoption trends upward
- Success Validation & Handoff (1–2 sessions): prove ROI, deliver documentation, transition ownership to internal team

### IC Role Shift
Builder → Enabler → Strategic Consultant
- Deliver tailored training sessions for Champions and end-users
- Provide prompt libraries and monitor initial user engagement
- Lead the closure meeting to validate ROI
- Provide complete handoff package: maintenance guides and support paths

### ADKAR Change Management Questions
- **Engagement:** Are users actively using AI features, and is the output accurate?
- **Awareness:** Does the team understand exactly how their workflow has changed?
- **Desire:** Can every user identify how these AI changes make their individual jobs easier?
- **Ability:** Is the team skilled enough to use AI tools effectively without constant help?
- **Reinforcement:** What is our plan to keep this momentum going long-term?

### Success Validation & Closure Questions
- **Goal Alignment:** Does the final solution hit the targets we aligned on?
- **Effort Reduction:** Have we measurably reduced manual tasks and repetitive work?
- **Independence:** Is your team confident enough to manage these AI features on their own?
- **Value Realization:** What is the biggest win the team has seen from these AI configurations?
- **Future Growth:** What's the next area of the business you want to optimize?

### Success Definition
Verifiable efficiency gains + reduced manual effort + a team that confidently manages intelligent workflows WITHOUT consultant intervention.

### Adopt Checklist
- Adoption: users actively engaging with new AI features independently?
- Value Validation: customer confirmed solution met original goals set at Assess phase?
- Sustainability: customer identified internal owners to maintain AI tools moving forward?
- Final Sign-off: delivered all training and documentation, received formal sign-off?

### Why the Adopt Phase Matters
Prevents "forever projects" — protects IC capacity from becoming a permanent support resource. Validates actual impact: proves the business is measurably better off than when you started while ensuring the customer has the independence to maintain that success long-term.
